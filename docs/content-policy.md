# Content and evidence policy

This policy governs every work profile in the prototype.

## Publication rules

1. Identify the relationship accurately as an owned product, owned initiative, Cynbit-published case study, or independently corroborated client engagement.
2. Attach source IDs to every substantive factual claim.
3. Reserve a verified-client label for work corroborated by the client or supported by written publication authorization and evidence.
4. Attribute a project, technology, date, status, and result to Cynbit when only a Cynbit-controlled source supports it.
5. Never infer a project-specific technology stack from Cynbit's company-wide capability list.
6. Classify numerical outcomes as measured, reported, target, or unavailable.
7. Require a listed source for every measured, reported, or target value.
8. Add a visible caveat to every result supported only by first-party material.
9. Display missing implementation and outcome information instead of filling gaps with generic copy.
10. Prefer original summaries linked to sources; avoid unnecessary quotations.
11. Do not use client logos, screenshots, testimonials, or proprietary assets without documented permission.

## Evidence levels

- **Corroborated:** supported by Cynbit and an independent client, partner, registry, publication, or equivalent source.
- **Owned and documented:** identified as Cynbit-owned work in official public channels.
- **Cynbit-published, independently unconfirmed:** a project story found in Cynbit's official case-study library without client-side corroboration. The interface may summarize it as Cynbit's published account but cannot present it as independently verified.
- **Unsupported or fictional:** excluded from the company-focused catalog.

The four owned profiles use the owned-and-documented level. The thirteen official project pages use the first-party published level. BitElevate also cites one third-party credential record, limited to the one completion it describes.

## Maintenance checklist

- Re-open every external reference and update its review date.
- Confirm every claim's source IDs still resolve and are listed on that profile.
- Review additions for unsupported relationships, clients, technologies, dates, quotations, and metrics.
- Confirm targets are not styled or phrased as achieved results.
- Confirm every first-party metric retains both attribution and its methodology caveat.
- Re-run catalog validation and the full automated suite.
- Review the rendered limitations and source list on each detail page.
- Record changed or unavailable sources in `content-evidence.md` before release.
