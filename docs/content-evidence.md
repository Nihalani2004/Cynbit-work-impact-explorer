## Claims deliberately excluded or qualified

- Former fictional clients, outcomes, and technologies are removed.
- Company-wide technologies are not assigned to a project unless its own source names them.
- Targets and unexplained scores on owned-product pages are not presented as achievements.
- First-party project metrics are shown only as “Outcome reported by Cynbit” with a visible caveat that methodology, baseline, sample size, and client confirmation were not located.
- No organization logo, project screenshot, testimonial, or client asset is reproduced.

## Admission gate for stronger verification

A published study may be upgraded to a verified client engagement only after public client-side corroboration or written confirmation and publication permission. A metric may be labelled measured only when its source, timeframe, baseline, population, and measurement context are available. Until then, the current first-party label and caveat must remain.
